# GeneEd visual assets

The DNA, pea-plant and laboratory images in `dist/assets/` were generated with OpenAI ImageGen for this prototype on 12 October 2026. They are AI-generated editorial assets, not source photography or experimental evidence. Original renders are preserved separately; the project includes optimized JPEG copies.

- dna.jpg: realistic molecular illustration; not a microscopy image or scale model.
- peas.jpg: photographic-style illustration of purple pea flowers and pods.
- lab.jpg: photographic-style biology laboratory scene; no wet-lab activity is implemented.
- Logo: original editable SVG helix mark, embedded in index.html.

Theme: cobalt #234DB4, navy #172642, coral #D66B59 and soft blue surfaces. Informational text maintains a dark foreground; coral is a decorative accent.
